Our Story

Purpose-Driven Creative Media Production

Emmanuel Africana Creatives is a media production brand built on creativity, excellence, and purposeful storytelling. With hands-on experience in live streaming direction, photography, videography, content creation, and editing, the brand helps individuals, organizations, churches, and businesses communicate their message with clarity and impact.

Based in Eldoret, Kenya, Emmanuel has freelanced with Qreate Media, Telo Photography, and Qip Photography, created digital content for Open Conversation with Nesh, currently serves as a content creator and editor at Sirikwa Pentecostal Fellowship Church, and volunteered as Stream Director for Light Up Eldoret.

Every frame captured, every stream directed, and every edit crafted carries a commitment to telling stories that matter — with authenticity, technical precision, and creative boldness.
